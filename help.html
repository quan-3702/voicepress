How to use VoicePress

1. Open a book

Click 📂 Open a book and choose a Word (.docx), PDF, EPUB, text (.txt), RTF or web page (.html) file – or drag the file onto the window. You can also click 📋 Paste text and paste anything you copied. VoicePress finds the chapters by itself (from the headings in Word, the table of contents in EPUB and PDF, or lines like “Chapter 1” or “Chương 1”). Untick include on chapters you do not want, such as the copyright page.

PDFs must have real text (you can select words in them). Scanned pages are pictures and cannot be read. Ebooks with copy protection (DRM) and Kindle files cannot be opened. Old .doc files: open them in Word and save as .docx.

2. Choose a voice

Pick the language and a voice on the left and click ▶ Try this voice. VoicePress guesses the language of your book. The first time you use a voice it is downloaded once (about 20–115 MB); after that it works without internet. Voices marked best quality sound the most natural but are bigger and a little slower. Set the speed and how long the pause between sentences is.

3. Make the audiobook

Click 🎧 Make the audiobook. The AI voice runs on your own PC, so it takes a while: a typical PC makes one hour of audio in about 10–20 minutes (Pro uses more processor cores and is up to 3× faster). You can keep working, listen to the chapters that are ready, or click ⏹ Stop and go on another day – nothing that is made is lost. Keep your PC plugged in for long books.

4. Listen and read along

Choose a chapter and press ▶ (or the Space bar). The sentence being read is highlighted. Click any sentence to start from there. Change the playback speed with 1×.

5. Save MP3 files

⬇ MP3 saves the chapter on screen. When the whole book is made, ⬇ Save as one MP3 saves one file with chapter marks, and ⬇ Save chapters (ZIP) saves one MP3 per chapter plus a playlist – unzip it and copy the folder to your phone, a USB stick for the car, or an MP3 player.

6. Fix mistakes

✏️ Edit text changes the text of a chapter (only the sentences you change are made again) and can split a chapter in two. With Pro, ☰ More → Fix how words are said lets you write how a name or short form should be read, one per line: Dr. = Doctor, TP.HCM = thành phố Hồ Chí Minh.

Free and Pro

Free: every file type, all 30+ languages and all voices, listen and read along, save MP3 files, 3,000 words of audio a day (about 20 minutes) and 2 books in your library. Pro (one-time purchase, no subscription): whole books with no daily limit, up to 3× faster, unlimited books, fix how words are said.

Your books stay private

Your files are read and spoken on this PC. Nothing is uploaded. Audio is kept in the app's storage until you delete the book (🗑).
