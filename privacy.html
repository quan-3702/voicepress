VoicePress privacy policy

VoicePress works entirely on your own computer. The books and texts you open, and the audio made from them, are kept only on your PC (in the app's own storage) and in files you choose to save. Nothing you open is uploaded, collected or shared.

The first time you use a voice, the app downloads the voice model and the speech engine from public open-source servers (Hugging Face and jsDelivr). Only these program files are downloaded; none of your text is sent.

The app has no account, no advertising and no tracking.

Purchases of VoicePress Pro are handled by the Microsoft Store.
